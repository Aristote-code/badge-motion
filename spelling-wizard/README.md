# Spelling Wizard badge (A-B-C tiles + magic swoosh)

`spelling-wizard-badge.svg` is the artwork (generated by `gen.py`). `spelling-wizard-badge.html` animates whatever is in it (same workflow as the other badges).

## Edit → see it animate

1. `node studio.mjs` and open http://localhost:5193.
2. Edit `spelling-wizard-badge.svg` and save over it; the animation reloads. Click the badge to replay with sound.
3. From Figma, tick **Include "id" attribute** when exporting SVG, or just say "I updated it in Figma" and it can be pulled from the frame.

## Export

- `node render.mjs` → intro video (8.2 s) + seamless idle loop (4 s), both with sound, plus PNG, WAV, `.ahap`, Android Kotlin, haptics JSON
- `node render.mjs --extras` → only the sound + haptics files

## The choreography

| Time | What happens | Sound | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | medal pops in | marimba boop | tap |
| 0.16 | ribbons drop and swing | soft double flap | light tap |
| 0.64 / 0.94 / 1.24 | tiles A, B, C drop in one by one from ~70 px above their spot inside the face, fading and growing in (drops start at 0.42 / 0.72 / 1.02), squash on landing, small bounce and a rotation settle | woody clack + the tile "says" its note with a rising glide: C5, E5, G5 | tap per tile, getting stronger |
| 1.44 → 1.84 | C turns over on its own axis: the cream face squeezes to an edge, the gold face opens out with an overshoot (swap at 1.64) | upward "fwip" + bright marimba and bell on the reveal | tap at the reveal |
| 1.98 → 2.86 | the swoosh draws on like a wand trail: back half first (behind the tiles), then the front half (in front) in one continuous stroke | quick rising harp glissando with a soft shimmer | light buzz (rising) |
| ≈2.4 → 2.75 | the star rides the head of the front half, spinning, and settles on its spot on the swoosh | (part of the shimmer) | — |
| 2.88 | star pops; burst dashes shoot out from C's corner (2.90 / 2.95 / 3.00) | bell | crisp tap |
| 3.16 | complete: rings, confetti, light rays | "ta-da!" chord | strong hit + ticks |
| 3.24 | sparkle stars burst in | tings | — |
| 3.38 | shine sweep | harp glissando | — |
| idle (4 s) | tiles hop in a wave A → B → C; a glint runs along the swoosh (back then front) and the star flashes as it passes; star twinkles; burst dashes flick out; rays turn | shine once per loop | — |

Timings live in `T` near the top of the script in `spelling-wizard-badge.html` (idle glint window in `GLINT`); sounds and haptics in `buildCues`.

## Layer names

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| `badge`, `medal`, `ribbon-left`, `ribbon-right`, `sparkles` (`star-1`, `star-2`) | as in the other badges |
| `spelling` | the whole icon group (tiles, swoosh, star, burst) |
| `tiles` | each child is a tile (`tile-a`, `tile-b`, `tile-c`); they drop in left → right by position, whatever their stacking order (B is stacked behind A and C) |
| `tile-c` | the flipping tile; turns over around its own centre |
| `tile-c-cream` / `tile-c-gold` | the two faces of C; cream shows before the flip, gold after (cream is hidden in the static artwork) |
| `swoosh-back` / `swoosh-front` | the ring behind the tiles (top) and in front (bottom); each is revealed along its guide |
| `swoosh-back-path` / `swoosh-front-path` | invisible centre lines of each half: the draw-on direction and the glint path |
| `magic-star` | rides the front half and stops at the point of the line nearest its own position, then pops |
| `burst` | each child (`dash-1..3`) shoots out from the C tile's centre direction |
