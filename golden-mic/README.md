# Golden Mic badge (gold capsule microphone on a yoke stand)

`golden-mic-badge.svg` is the artwork (generated by `gen.py`). `golden-mic-badge.html` animates whatever is in it (same workflow as the other badges).

## Edit → see it animate

1. `node studio.mjs` and open http://localhost:5188.
2. Edit `golden-mic-badge.svg` and save over it; the animation reloads. Click the badge to replay with sound.
3. From Figma, tick **Include "id" attribute** when exporting SVG, or just say "I updated it in Figma" and it can be pulled from the frame.

## Export

- `node render.mjs` → intro video (8.9 s) + seamless idle loop (4 s), both with sound, plus PNG, WAV, `.ahap`, Android Kotlin, haptics JSON
- `node render.mjs --extras` → only the sound + haptics files

## The choreography

| Time | What happens | Sound | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | medal pops in | marimba boop | tap |
| 0.16 | ribbons drop and swing | soft double flap | light tap |
| 0.40 | round base pops in (squash and stretch) | soft low thump | tap |
| 0.62 → 1.02 | stem + yoke grow up out of the base socket, overshoot and settle | rising glide → woody note | building buzz + landing tap |
| 1.08 → 1.36 | mic fades in just above the yoke (~70 px, inside the face) and drops in, tilted | soft falling whistle | — |
| 1.36 | mic lands on the pivots; the stand jolts, the knobs "click" | woody clunk + click | firm tap |
| 1.36 → 2.0 | mic rocks upright on its pivots with a damped bounce | two-note seat click (1.66) | light tap |
| 2.04 / 2.22 | "tap tap": the mic dips under a fingertip | muffled taps | taps |
| 2.44 / 2.70 / 2.96 / 3.24 | mic sings a 4-note melody (G5 C6 E6 G6): it bobs and leans to the singing side; the wave fans pop in and pulse outward on each note, left, right, left, then **both** on the held top note | sung triangle/sine voice with a scoop and vibrato | light tap per note (+ soft buzz on the last) |
| 3.70 | gem sparkles (pulse + spinning glint) | two tings | light tap |
| 3.92 | complete: rings, confetti, light rays | "ta-da!" chord | strong hit + ticks |
| 4.0 | sparkle stars burst in | tings | — |
| 4.14 | shine sweep | harp glissando | — |
| idle | mic sways gently on its pivot; wave fans pulse every second (L, R, L, R) with a little mic bob; the gem glint twinkles once per loop; rays turn | shine once per loop | — |

Timings live in `T` near the top of the script in `golden-mic-badge.html` (and `middle.js`); sounds in `sounds.js`; sounds and haptics are cued in `buildCues`.

## Layer names

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| `badge`, `medal`, `ribbon-left`, `ribbon-right`, `sparkles` | as in the other badges |
| `base` | the round base; pops in from its bottom edge |
| `stand` | stem + yoke + gem; grows up from the bottom of `stem` (the base socket) |
| `stem`, `yoke` | parts of the stand (`stem`'s bottom edge sets where the stand grows from) |
| `gem` (+ `gem-glint`) | the purple gem at the yoke bottom; pulses, glint spins in and twinkles |
| `mic` | the microphone; drops in, rocks and sways around the pivot (the midpoint between the two knobs) |
| `mic-grille` | the grille slots; its centre is the origin the wave dashes pulse away from |
| `knobs` | each child (`knob-left`, `knob-right`) is a pivot knob drawn over the mic; they ride with the stand and click on landing |
| `waves-left`, `waves-right` | each child is a sound-wave dash (`wave-l1..3`, `wave-r1..3`); they pulse outward from the mic head |
