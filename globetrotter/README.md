# Globetrotter badge (globe + pins + route + orbiting plane)

`globetrotter-badge.svg` is the artwork (generated by `gen.py`). `globetrotter-badge.html` animates whatever is in it (same workflow as the other badges).

## Edit → see it animate

1. `node studio.mjs` and open http://localhost:5192.
2. Edit `globetrotter-badge.svg` and save over it; the animation reloads. Click the badge to replay with sound.
3. From Figma, tick **Include "id" attribute** when exporting SVG, or just say "I updated it in Figma" and it can be pulled from the frame.

## Export

- `node render.mjs` → intro video (9.4 s) + seamless idle loop (4 s), both with sound, plus PNG, WAV, `.ahap`, Android Kotlin, haptics JSON
- `node render.mjs --extras` → only the sound + haptics files

## The choreography

| Time | What happens | Sound | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | medal pops in | marimba boop | tap |
| 0.16 | ribbons drop and swing | soft double flap | light tap |
| 0.28 | globe pops in | round woody "boop" | tap |
| 0.32 → 1.4 | continents spin west → east inside the globe (wrapping seamlessly), easing to a stop | soft rising whirr with a slowing flutter | light fading buzz |
| 1.22 → 1.48 | pin 1 drops onto its tip, squashes and bounces | "plink" C6 | tap |
| 1.6 → 2.0 | dashed route draws dash by dash from pin 1, sagging across the globe to pin 2 | a soft climbing tick per dash | light ticks |
| 2.12 → 2.38 | pin 2 drops onto the orbit, where the route joins it | "plink" G6 | tap |
| 2.5 | plane pops up on pin 2, pointing along the route | little two-note lift | light tap |
| 2.6 → 4.32 | plane flies once round the globe along the orbit and on to its rest at the top right; the orbit ring is drawn as its trail; on the back half it passes behind the globe (smaller, dimmer) | smooth sine glide with gentle vibrato, quieter behind the globe, panning with the plane | light buzz |
| 4.32 | plane arrives | landing bell | tap |
| 4.42 | complete: rings, confetti, light rays | "ta-da!" chord | strong hit + ticks |
| 4.5 | sparkle stars burst in | tings | — |
| 4.64 | shine sweep | harp glissando | — |
| idle | continents sway gently and return, plane bobs back and forth along a short stretch of its path, pins hop in turn, stars twinkle, rays turn | shine once per loop | — |

Timings live in `T` near the top of the script in `globetrotter-badge.html` (source: `middle.js`); sounds in `sounds.js`; sounds and haptics cues in `buildCues`.

## Layer names

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| `badge`, `medal`, `ribbon-left`, `ribbon-right`, `sparkles` | as in the other badges |
| `globe` (+ `globe-body`) | pops in from the centre of `globe-body` |
| `continents` | the land strip; its children are copies one period apart (the period is measured from them); scrolls sideways inside the globe clip |
| `ring-back` | orbit half behind the globe (drawn under it, dimmer) |
| `ring-front` | orbit half in front of the globe; both halves run in the plane's direction and are revealed as its trail |
| `route` | each child is a dash; they appear in order from pin 1 to pin 2 |
| `pin-1`, `pin-2` | drop onto their tips (bottom centre) |
| `pin-shadows` | one shadow per pin, grows as its pin lands |
| `flight-path` | invisible guide: pin 2 → once round the orbit → plane rest; the plane is placed along it, nose along the tangent |
| `plane` | rests at the end of `flight-path`; moves behind `globe` while on the back half |
