# Grammar Guardian badge (winged shield + "!")

Artwork follows `reference/grammar-guardian-reference.svg` (overlaid at 0.72 scale): each angel wing is a fan of banana-shaped crescent feathers that all grow from one root low beside the shield and nest inside each other. The front, lightest one hugs the shield and rises to a tip beside the star, the ones behind fan further out, the small dark ones point out and down, and every tip hooks upward. The "!" is the chunky reference size.

`grammar-guardian-badge.svg` is the artwork (generated by `gen.py`). `grammar-guardian-badge.html` animates whatever is in it (same workflow as the other badges).

## Edit → see it animate

1. `node studio.mjs` and open http://localhost:5187.
2. Edit `grammar-guardian-badge.svg` (or `gen.py` and run `python3 gen.py`) and save; the animation reloads. Click the badge to replay with sound.
3. From Figma, tick **Include "id" attribute** when exporting SVG, or just say "I updated it in Figma" and it can be pulled from the frame.

## Export

- `node render.mjs` → intro video (8.3 s) + seamless idle loop (4 s), both with sound, plus PNG, WAV, `.ahap`, Android Kotlin, haptics JSON
- `node render.mjs --extras` → only the sound + haptics files

## The choreography

| Time | What happens | Sound | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | medal pops in | marimba boop | tap |
| 0.16 | ribbons drop and swing | soft double flap | light tap |
| 0.38 | shield pops in | low woody "boop" | tap |
| 0.72 → 1.27 | wings unfurl from behind the shield, rotating open about their shoulders | airy whoosh + quick rising plucks fanning left/right | soft rising buzz |
| 0.84 / 0.95 / 1.06 | feather rows fan out one after another: coverts → secondaries → primaries | (same) | — |
| 1.52 / 1.88 | two wing flaps (rows ripple behind the wing) | soft woody "fwup" | light buzz |
| 2.22 → 2.52 | "!" bar drops in from above, lands with a bouncy squash | bright bell "ding" (E6) | crisp tap |
| 2.67 | the dot lands under it | higher "ding" (A6) | crisp tap |
| 2.92 | burst dashes shoot out, star spins in | sparkly plucks + glint | two light ticks |
| 3.3 | complete: rings, confetti, light rays | "ta-da!" chord | strong hit + ticks |
| 3.38 | star sparkles again | tings | — |
| 3.52 | shine sweep | harp glissando | — |
| idle | wings give one gentle flap per loop, "!" bobs, dashes pulse, star twinkles, rays turn | shine once per loop | — |

Timings live in `T` near the top of the script in `grammar-guardian-badge.html` (source: `middle.js`); sounds in `sounds.js`; cues/haptics in `buildCues`.

## Layer names

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| `badge`, `medal`, `ribbon-left`, `ribbon-right`, `sparkles` | as in the other badges (`sparkles` holds the gold `star`) |
| `shield` | pops in; dips when the "!" lands |
| `wing-left`, `wing-right` | the whole wing; unfurls and flaps about its pivot |
| `wing-left-pivot`, `wing-right-pivot` | small circle at the wing root, low on the shield's flank (hidden behind it) = rotation point |
| `wing-*-coverts`, `wing-*-secondaries`, `wing-*-primaries` | feather tiers, front → back: 1 light covert (the tall front feather beside the star), 2 lavender secondaries, 3 small deep-purple primaries; each child is a crescent feather that fans out from the top feather of its tier |
| `exclaim` | the whole "!" (idle bob) |
| `exclaim-bar`, `exclaim-dot` | drop in one after the other and squash from their bottom edge |
| `burst` | each child dash shoots outward from the star |
