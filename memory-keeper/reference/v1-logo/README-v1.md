# Memory Keeper badge (purple hexagon + elephant)

Elephants never forget. `memory-keeper-badge.svg` is the artwork, generated by `gen.py`. `memory-keeper-badge.html` animates it, using the same workflow as the other badges.

## Artwork (follows the reference)

The artwork follows `reference/memory-keeper-reference.svg`. The elephant curves are taken straight from the reference, mapped at 0.72 scale so the reference hexagon centre (626.5, 622) lands on the badge centre (420, 480). They are then split into parts that can move on their own:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| ear | behind the head; flat top, round lobe |
| head | dome, flat face front, and the tusk sweeping left under the mouth slit |
| trunk | one spiral: down from the face, round the bottom, up the right and curling in over the top |
| eye | the eye |

The dark "C" is the gap between the curled tip and the trunk. All four parts share one gradient, so the joins are invisible.

The frame is a rounded hexagon matching the reference: a dark outline, a lighter purple bevel, and an inner face with a soft diagonal sheen. There are no ribbons, and the badge is centred at (420, 480).

Only one correction was made: the ear's razor-sharp top-right corner (a crop artifact) is softened slightly.

## Edit → see it animate

1. Run `node studio.mjs` and open http://localhost:5194.
2. Edit `memory-keeper-badge.svg` and save over it. The animation reloads.

## Export

`node render.mjs` writes:
- the intro video (3.9 s) and a seamless 4 s idle loop, both with sound
- PNG and WAV
- `.ahap`, Android Kotlin and haptics JSON

## The choreography

| Time | What happens | Sound | Haptic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | hexagon pops in | marimba boop | tap |
| 0.34 → 0.86 | elephant rises into the hexagon from below and lands with a squash | low rising glide, soft "thump" | tap + echo |
| 0.78 → 1.28 | ear unfolds from behind the head (hinged at its root), overshoots and flaps | airy flap + two woody notes | soft buzz + tap |
| 1.12 → 1.66 | head tilts back while the trunk gathers | rising pluck run | — |
| 1.66 | trunk puffs out: "toot-toot!" | friendly two-note trumpet | buzz + tap |
| 1.68 / 1.92 / 2.16 | three gold music notes drift out of the trunk into the dark face below the ear | G5 / B5 / D6 plinks | light ticks |
| 2.5 | knowing wink | tiny "tink" | tap |
| 2.9 | complete: rings, confetti, light rays | "ta-da!" chord | strong hit + ticks |
| 3.12 | shine sweep | harp glissando | — |
| idle (4 s) | ear fans gently, trunk sways, head breathes, one blink per loop (1.2 s), shine (2.6 s) | blink tink, shine | — |

Timings live in `T` near the top of `middle.js`, and sounds in `sounds.js`. Rebuild from `badges/` with:

```bash
python3 _kit/assemble.py memory-keeper memory-keeper/middle.js memory-keeper/sounds.js --title "Memory Keeper" --intro 3.92 --layers badge,medal,medal-outline,elephant,ear,head,trunk,eye,sparkles --port 5194
```

The trunk deliberately doesn't swing far. It's cut from one silhouette with the head, so a big rotation would open a seam. It gathers and puffs instead.

## Layer names

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| `badge`, `medal` (`medal-outline`, `medal-face`) | the hexagon frame; it pops in and squashes at the finale |
| `elephant` | the whole head; rises in, lands, tilts back to trumpet, breathes |
| `ear` | unfolds from behind the head about its root, then fans in the loop |
| `head` | dome + face + tusk. Its hidden lower part fills in behind the trunk |
| `trunk` | gathers and puffs on the toot, then sways gently |
| `eye` | wink, then one blink per loop |
| `sparkles` | empty (the reference has no stars) |
